# Thirty-minute course sample

**Mount Etna: life on a living volcano** is the one fully written long demo course.
Its four lessons contain roughly 2,800 words of Markdown and reflection activities. Each has
three questions; the final summary quiz has six, for 18 questions in total.

The planned workload is 30 minutes: 22 minutes reading and reflecting across the
four lessons (6 + 6 + 5 + 5), about one minute for each lesson quiz, and four
minutes for the final quiz. This is an estimate, not a timer or minimum dwell
requirement. The product target is **20–40 minutes for an entire course**, rather
than for each lesson. Other sample courses retain short placeholder text; their
duration metadata is normalised to the same target range, except the deliberate
missing-metadata case. No other full-length courses are generated.

`course.cjs` describes the course, durations and answerable questions. The four
Markdown files are original teaching text using headings, emphasis, tables,
callouts, reflection checklists and expandable worked examples. See the
[Markdown contract](../MARKDOWN.md) for supported syntax. Fictional
field comparisons and reflection exercises are explicitly identified as such.
The mobile reader displays these as separate sections and activity cards.

Factual background was checked against primary sources:

- [UNESCO: Mount Etna](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1427/) — location, volcanic landforms and World Heritage context.
- [Etna Park: environment and territory](https://parcoetna.it/lambiente-ed-il-territorio/) — vegetation varies with substrate, altitude and exposure.
- [NPS: volcanic landscape succession](https://parkplanning.nps.gov/showFile.cfm?projectID=11027&sfid=15980) — background examples of colonisation and microsites; not evidence about a specific Etna plot.

The exercises and explanations are sample courseware, not a claim of expert review.
They are stored in the normal lesson/question/answer tables, without an LLM call.
The optional seed grants this free course to Demo Learner without spending or
crediting coins, and creates no enrollment until the learner starts it. Existing
courses, attempts and completed history remain intact. Re-running the seed does
not replace attempted content. `legacy-text-hashes.json` identifies the exact
previous demo text bodies for the formatting-only upgrade; edited bodies remain
untouched. See [the learning guide](../../../LEARNING.md).
